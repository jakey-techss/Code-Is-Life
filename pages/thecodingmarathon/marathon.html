01 / THE IDEA WHY I'M DOING THIS

More than just
writing code.

The Coding Marathon is a personal engineering challenge built around one simple idea: the best way to become a better engineer is to keep building.

Over 24 months, I'm challenging myself to create 96 products across different areas of software development. Each month brings four new builds, each with its own problem to solve, technologies to explore, and lessons to take away.

This isn't just about finishing projects. It's about learning how to plan, design systems, work through bugs, make technical decisions, and turn ideas into working products.

Every project and every day of progress becomes part of the record. The wins, the setbacks, and everything in between.
